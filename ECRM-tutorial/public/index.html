Section 01 / Business Explain

How ECRM Works

Complete overview of the ECRM consumer engagement platform — campaign operations, field force activities, supervisor monitoring, and data reporting pipeline.

🚫

Why This Business Exists

Smoking advertisements are not allowed in public media or billboards. Direct person-to-person consumer reach is mandatory. The only effective way to contact smokers is by visiting outlets.

⚙️

How the Business is Run

Multiple campaigns run with survey questions. RAs (Retail Agents) visit outlets & interact one-on-one with consumers (≥18 years). They collect data & preferences, which are used to understand behavior & drive future campaigns.

🎯 Objectives: Collect consumer & product-related data · surveys as primary engagement & insight tool · collect & analyze historical data · competitor & internal product analysis · design and execute requirement-based, campaign-wise marketing flows.

📊 Market & Product Analysis

🏆

Competitor Analysis

Tracks competitor product offerings, promotions, & campaign trends. Identifies market positioning, gaps, and opportunities via eCRM.

📦

Own Product Analysis

Tracks sales trends, customer engagement levels, survey feedback, and current product lifecycle stages.

✨

New Product Promotion

Identifies target audiences, validates pre-launch via surveys, runs pilot campaigns, and tracks performance post-launch.

📈

Expected Benefits

Better customer understanding, reduced dependency on traditional ads, data-driven decisions, improved product-market fit, & higher ROI.

👥 User vs Consumer

User: Person who uses the eCRM app to conduct surveys or manage campaigns (RA, AC, SUP).

Consumer: Person whose data is collected through surveys.

🏷️ Product Classifications

Franchise Core BATB brands (British American Tobacco Bangladesh).

SOB Non-BATB products (competitor or external products).

🗂️ Location Hierarchy & PTR

Hierarchy: Region → Area → Cluster → Outlet

  • Terminal point for contact: Outlet (all consumer/retailer contacts occur here).
  • Campaign terminal point: Typically POI; sometimes Cluster.
  • Outlet, Retailer, and POS (Point of Sale) refer to the same entity.
  • Location is assigned to a cluster.
🎁 PTR (Purchase Through Retail)

Represents rewards/gifts offered during a campaign. Gift = PTR = Reward. Stored in the materials table (linked to Contacts). Also defines Promotional Material Templates.

How campaign flow works

This section explains the operational campaign blocks used inside the eCRM campaign engine. These components are used by Business Teams, Campaign Managers, Supervisors, and Technical Teams during campaign creation, survey design, consumer engagement flow configuration, PTR execution, AV engagement, and reporting workflow management.

Step-by-step logic:

  1. Initial Setup: The system automatically triggers the audio_start to log compliance.
  2. Consumer Input: Logic gates like dropdown, radio, or contactNo capture target demographics.
  3. Validation: Verification steps (like age checking) block invalid flows.
  4. Execution: Successful targets receive rewards/PTRs via the final outcome components.

Section 02 / SQL Reports

Report Documentation

Full SQL queries with clause-by-clause explanations, join maps, and schema mappings for all four production reports.

📋

Daily Contact Survey Report

ecrm.contacts (c) · Sample: c.id = 12429776

Generates a comprehensive per-contact record combining contact metadata, full 7-level location hierarchy, consumer profile (name, age, address, occupation), brand information, UTC→Asia/Dhaka timezone-corrected timing, and pivoted Bengali-language survey answers. One row per unique contact event.

ecrm.contactsecrm.locationsecrm.user_infos ecrm.campaignsecrm.consumersecrm.products contact_survey_data_maps
👁️

Live Observation Report

ecrm.joint_calls (jc) · Sample: jc.id = 444487

Records a supervisor's real-time observation of a BR conducting a consumer contact. Evaluates process compliance across 16 checklist items (correct cluster, correct attire, shop permission, device compliance, and full campaign messaging). Cross-schema join with apsis_data via name-based match. One row per joint call observation.

ecrm.joint_callsecrm.locationsecrm.users ecrm.user_infosecrm.campaigns apsis_data.distributorspointjoint_calls_survey_data_maps
📞

Call Checkback Report

ecrm.supervisor_contacts (sc) · Sample: sc.id = 495011

Post-contact quality audit where supervisors call consumers to verify BR performance. Links back to the original BR contact via a 4-key composite join (phone + BR ID + campaign ID + date). Uses ID-based cross-schema join with apsis_data (more reliable than Live Observation's name-based match). 17 survey verification questions.

ecrm.supervisor_contactsecrm.contactsecrm.locations ecrm.usersecrm.user_infosecrm.campaigns apsis_data.distributorspointsupervisor_contact_survey_data_maps
☎️

Call Center Report

ecrm.consumer_dialer_contacts (cdc) · Sample: cdc.id = 493001

Tracks outbound call center dialer contacts. Unique architecture: anchors at Cluster (type=7) via cdl.region_id — NOT outlet. Uses a 3-step table chain (cdc → cdba → cdl). Agency derived from region name via CASE expression. English-language survey questions. Includes contact status logic (Successful / Completed / Pending).

Section 04 / ER Diagram

Database Entity Relationship Diagram

Interactive ER diagram for the ECRM production database (ecrm_prod). Zoom, pan, and explore all table relationships across both schemas.

📌 How to use: Use +/− buttons or mouse wheel to zoom. Click and drag to pan. The SVG diagram contains the complete ecrm_prod schema generated from the live database.
⚠️ Key Architectural Notes:
• ecrm.locations is a self-referential table — type 1=Region through 8=Outlet, joined by parent column.
• Call Center anchors at Cluster (type=7) via cdl.region_id — the column name is misleading (it stores cluster ID, not region ID).
• Cross-schema joins: Live Obs uses l_point.name = dp.name (name-match). Checkback uses po.source_id = dp.id (ID-match — more reliable).
• Survey data is stored in separate maps tables per report type — never mix join keys across report types.
